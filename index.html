PokeradarTCG

Pokémon TCG price comparison · Solo-built · May 2026 – present

Compares prices of Pokémon TCG products across Spanish online stores, running 24/7 on my own Linux server.

  • Python/Flask backend with 97 scrapers feeding a SQLite database (WAL mode).
  • Linux VPS (Hetzner) with nginx, systemd and cron; TLS and health checks with email alerts.
  • Daily, weekly and monthly backups with verified restores.
  • Push/email notifications, Umami analytics and automated social posting.
  • pytest + Playwright test suite, ≥80% coverage per file.
PythonFlaskLinuxnginxsystemdSQLitePlaywright

How it runs

cron→ 97 scrapers→ SQLite (WAL)→ Flask + gunicorn→ nginx (TLS)→ users
cron 03:00→ backup (daily / weekly / monthly)→ rsync to Hetzner Storage Box→ restore check
health check after every scrape run→ email alert

Trainer Academy

Pokémon VGC analytics · Final degree project, kept in production · Oct 2024 – present

Collects and analyzes competitive Pokémon matches to produce statistics, a damage calculator and an AI turn assistant.
